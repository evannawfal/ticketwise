# Getting started with Northpine

Northpine is a shared scheduling and task board for small teams. This guide walks a new workspace owner through setup.

## Create your workspace

Sign up at app.northpine.example with your work email. The first person to sign up becomes the workspace **Owner**. Owners can change billing, delete the workspace, and transfer ownership.

Pick a workspace URL (for example `acme.northpine.example`). You can change it later under Settings > Workspace > URL, but old links will stop working after 30 days.

## Invite your team

Go to Settings > Members > Invite. Paste up to 50 email addresses at once, separated by commas or new lines. Invites expire after 7 days. You can resend an expired invite from the Pending tab.

New members join with the **Member** role. Change roles later from the Members page.

## Create your first board

Click **New board** in the sidebar and choose a template (Sprint, Content calendar, or Blank). Boards can be private, shared with specific people, or visible to the whole workspace.

## Install the apps

Northpine has desktop apps for macOS and Windows and mobile apps for iOS and Android. Your boards sync across all devices. The mobile app needs iOS 16 or Android 10 or newer.
