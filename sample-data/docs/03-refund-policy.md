# Refund policy

## Annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of the purchase or renewal date. After 14 days, annual plans are not refundable, but you can turn off auto-renew so the plan ends at the end of the term.

## Monthly plans

Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. When you cancel, the workspace keeps paid features until the end of the current month.

## Accidental renewals

If an annual plan renewed and the workspace has had no activity since the renewal date, support can approve a full refund up to 30 days after renewal. Agents should check the activity log before approving.

## How to request a refund

The Owner or a Billing Admin must request the refund from the email address on the account, or by contacting support from inside the app. Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

## What support agents can approve

Support agents can approve refunds up to $500 that meet this policy. Anything above $500, or any exception to this policy, needs approval from a support lead.
