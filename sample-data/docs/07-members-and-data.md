# Managing members and data

## Roles

- **Owner**: full control, including billing and deleting the workspace. A workspace can have several Owners.
- **Admin**: manage members, boards, and integrations. Cannot change billing.
- **Billing Admin**: manage plans, invoices, and payment methods only.
- **Member**: create and edit boards they have access to.
- **Guest**: access only to boards they are invited to. Guests are free.

## Remove a member

Go to Settings > Members, open the member, and click **Remove from workspace**. Their tasks stay in place and are reassigned to "Unassigned". You can choose to transfer their private boards to another member before removing them.

## Transfer ownership

An Owner can make another member an Owner under Settings > Members > Change role. If the only Owner has left the company, an Admin can request an ownership transfer from support; we email the current Owner and approve the transfer after 7 days if there's no response.

## Export your data

Owners can export all boards, tasks, comments, and attachments under Settings > Workspace > Export. Exports are generated as a ZIP of JSON and CSV files and emailed as a download link that expires after 24 hours. Large workspaces can take up to 2 hours.

## Delete a workspace

Only Owners can delete a workspace, under Settings > Workspace > Delete. Deleted workspaces can be restored by support for 30 days. After 30 days, all data is permanently deleted.
