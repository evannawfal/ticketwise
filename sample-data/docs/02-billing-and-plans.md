# Billing and plans

## Plans

- **Free**: up to 5 members, 3 boards, 7 days of activity history.
- **Team**: $8 per member per month billed annually, or $10 billed monthly. Unlimited boards, 1 year of history, integrations.
- **Business**: $16 per member per month billed annually. Adds SSO (SAML), audit log, and priority support with a 4-business-hour first response time.

## How seats are billed

You pay for every active member in the workspace. Guests are free and do not use a seat. When you add a member in the middle of a billing period, we charge a prorated amount for the rest of that period on your next invoice. When you remove a member, the unused time is added as account credit, not refunded to your card.

## Change your plan

Owners and Billing Admins can change plans under Settings > Billing > Plan. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period, and you keep paid features until then.

If you downgrade to Free with more than 5 members, the workspace becomes read-only until you remove members or upgrade again.

## Invoices and receipts

Download invoices from Settings > Billing > Invoices. To add a VAT or GST number, company name, or billing address to future invoices, edit Settings > Billing > Billing details. We cannot change invoices that have already been issued.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Annual Business plans over $5,000 can pay by invoice with net-30 terms; contact sales to set this up.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry 3 times over 10 days and email the Owner and Billing Admins each time. After the last failed retry, the workspace becomes read-only. Updating the card under Settings > Billing > Payment method retries the charge right away and restores access within a few minutes.
